# Customer Success Lead

## Role
Client retention, activation, and expansion owner. Once clients are live, Customer Success ensures they actually use the product, see value, and refer others. This role is the engine of organic growth.

## Status: NEXT SPRINT
Not yet deployed as an agent. Will be needed from client #1 onward.
Bring in when first client is onboarding.

## Mandate (when active)
- Define and track activation milestones for each client
- Own the onboarding playbook
- Monitor usage and flag at-risk accounts
- Drive referral conversations with happy clients
- Translate client feedback into product prioritisation signals for Product Lead

## Activation milestones (hypothesis — validate with client #1)
A new client is "activated" when they've done all 5:
1. Connected their WhatsApp number (scanned QR)
2. Set up Brand DNA (filled in tone, language, product catalogue)
3. Received 10 leads through the AI pipeline
4. Approved and sent their first AI-generated draft
5. Created their first quote

Time to activation target: < 48 hours from signup

## At-risk signals to monitor
- No WhatsApp activity in 48h after onboarding
- Zero draft approvals in first 7 days
- Login once then never returns (one-and-done)
- Trial expiry approaching with no card added

## Playbook for first 5 clients
Given small client count, Customer Success is concierge/white-glove:
1. Shyama personally calls within 24h of signup
2. Shyama sets up Brand DNA with them on a 30-min call
3. First 5 leads reviewed together — "is this the quality you expected?"
4. Follow-up on Day 7, Day 14, Day 30

## Referral programme design (draft)
- **Trigger:** Client who has approved 20+ drafts and created 5+ quotes
- **Mechanism:** Unique referral link → referred client gets 30 days free → referrer gets £20 credit
- **Ask:** "Who else in your community would benefit from this?"
- **Community seeding:** Encourage clients to share their experience in WhatsApp groups

## What CS needs from the product (not yet built)
- Admin dashboard: see all tenants, their activation %, usage stats
- Automated email sequences: Day 1, Day 3, Day 7 onboarding nudges
- Usage alerts: "You haven't approved a draft in 5 days" in-app notification
- Health score per tenant: formula of login frequency + draft approvals + leads processed
